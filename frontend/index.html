      Publish a local story

      Private by default. Nothing leaves this browser until you explicitly consent and press Publish. Uploading uses mobile data. The curator access key is never stored on this device.

      Publishing is manual. Editing a local story does not update its published version; withdraw first to replace it. Do not clear this browser's data before withdrawing a published copy.

      Saved on this device

      After you stop recording, KasiVoices saves the story in browser storage automatically. Download a backup in case your browser clears it.

      No mobile data required

      Recordings and saved stories work offline after the app shell has been cached. Recordings are uploaded only after explicit consent and a publish action.

      Recording and offline caching require HTTPS or localhost. To test offline app loading, use a production build and load it online once.
