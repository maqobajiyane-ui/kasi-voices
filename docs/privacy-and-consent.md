# Community archive privacy and consent (design requirements)

- Seek storyteller consent before publication or synchronization.
- Offer choices for attribution, anonymity, and approximate versus omitted location.
- Do not collect precise GPS coordinates automatically.
- Provide a future deletion/withdrawal flow for locally stored and server-published content.
- Provide clear notices for recording, retention, and mobile data use.
- Assess South Africa's POPIA obligations before deploying any personal-data processing.

No privacy functionality is implemented in Phase 0.
